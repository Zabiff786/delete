Google Play Data Safety Policy Compliance

Omni Notes — Account & Data Deletion

In compliance with the Google Play User Data Policy, Omni Notes provides users with the ability to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated cloud and local data.

Permanent Data Deletion Notice: Once your account deletion request is processed, all notes, sketches, audio recordings, synced formulas, and user credentials will be permanently erased from our Cloud Firestore database and cannot be recovered.

1. How to Delete Directly Inside the App (Instant)

  1. Open the Omni Notes app.
  2. Tap your Account / Profile icon at the top right of the dashboard.
  3. Tap Delete Account & Cloud Data at the bottom of the dialog.
  4. Confirm by tapping Yes, Delete Everything. All cloud and local notes will be erased immediately.

2. Request Deletion via Web / Email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can submit an account deletion request by emailing our developer support:

  • Developer: Zabi.dev
  • Support Email: ffvspubg420@gmail.com
  • App Name: Omni Notes (Package: com.zabi.dev.omninotes)

Please include the Google account email address associated with your Omni Notes account. Your data will be deleted within 48 to 72 hours of verification.

3. Types of Data Deleted

  • User Account Credentials (Firebase Auth UID, email, display name)
  • All synchronized Notes (Text, Plaintext, Categories, Tags)
  • Vector Drawing Canvas Sketches
  • Audio Memos and Math solver formulas
  • Local offline Room cache
